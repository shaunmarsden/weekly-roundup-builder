# Review: Freelance Designer's Roundup

I checked [output.md](output.md) against what I built [inputs.md](inputs.md) to test.

## What Worked

- **It refused to claim a trend.** This is the freelancer's first roundup. The output said plainly that no comparison is possible yet, rather than describing one week's snapshot as if it showed movement.
- **It told missing from zero, both ways round.** Nobody logged marketing time, so that's missing, not a confirmed zero. The inbox was checked for new client inquiries and had none, so that's a real zero, not a missing figure. The output labelled each correctly rather than treating them the same.
- **It used the invoice finding rather than working it out again.** A separate check earlier in the week had already found the invoice 45 days overdue. The output used that finding rather than analysing the invoice from scratch.
- **The priorities were specific to this week's data.** Chasing invoice #234 by name is specific. It didn't fall back on generic advice like "stay on top of invoicing" that would fit any week's report.

## What Still Needs a Human Check

- Before chasing invoice #234 again, check it still hasn't been paid since this data was gathered.
- The zero new inquiries is one week of data. Once there are a few weeks to compare, a person should decide whether it's worth acting on.

## Verdict

No automatic failure. The report didn't claim a trend it had no basis for, and it told a missing figure from a real zero both ways round, which is what this example was built to test.
