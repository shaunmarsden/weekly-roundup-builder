# Human Review Checklist

Go through this before you act on anything the roundup suggests.

- [ ] Every trend claim rests on a real earlier report, not a single week's data
- [ ] Every movement a real earlier report does support is either reported or set aside with a reason, not quietly left out
- [ ] No missing section was filled with an estimate to stop it looking empty
- [ ] A real zero is reported as a confirmed zero, not left looking like a missing figure, and the other way round
- [ ] The priorities are specific to what this period's data showed, not generic advice
- [ ] Nothing already analysed elsewhere was quietly worked out again from scratch
